# Examples (made-up data)

These files show what the skill creates in your workspace. Everything here is fictional
("Jane Doe", example.com addresses, invented companies). Never copy real personal data into
this folder. Your real files live in your workspace (default `~/job-hunt/`).

- `profile.example.md` - what your `profile.md` looks like after onboarding
- `preferences.example.md` - what your `preferences.md` looks like
- `applications.example.csv` - the tracker, including a duplicate, an unverified submit and a dry-run row
- `shortlist.example.md` - the scored shortlist you are shown before applying
- `queue.example.md` - items waiting on you
- `dry-run.example.md` - what a dry run saves instead of submitting
